# DomainSpecific

A creative answer scoped to one domain, chosen from diverse convention-breaking candidates by that domain's own success criteria.

## Ideal State

- The winner comes from a set of diverse candidates that challenge the named domain's conventions, and the response names the runner-up it beat.
- Candidates break a real convention of the named domain, not a generic one.
- The winner is evaluated against the domain's success criteria instead of generic novelty (emotional impact and coherence for art, scalability and customer behavior for business, and so on), and the selection is justified against those criteria, not just "most creative."
- The surfaced answer is one the principal would not have written on the first pass, still fully answers the brief, and says why it beats the obvious answer.

## Anti-criteria

- The winning candidate is never chosen on generic novelty alone.
