# BUSINESS/

Notes about your company itself: what it sells, how it runs, and the catalogs the content tools read by name. This folder is yours and stays on your machine. LifeOS never publishes it, and the Daemon profile builder skips it on purpose.

## What goes here

- `YouTubeVideoTypes.md`: your catalog of video types (look, length, structure). The content pipeline gives each recording a type, and each type points at one section of this catalog by name.
- Anything else that describes the business as a whole: offerings, positioning, operating notes. Customer and engagement files belong in `WORK/`, not here.

## How to fill it in

Edit the files directly, or ask your DA to interview you about the business and write the results here. Keep the section names in `YouTubeVideoTypes.md` unchanged; the tools match on them.
