# StoryFilm preferences

Read by `skills/StoryFilm/Intents/Storyboard.md` before it writes the bible. Every section is optional; with none set, the story alone decides how people look and the tone of the shots.

## Casting

How to describe people where the story is silent. Example: "Write skin as a shade, not a race, and keep the cast varied."

## Register

One tone for every shot. Example: "Quiet, painterly, unhurried."

## Style precedent

Optional notes on a style that worked before. Example: "Watercolor on cream paper, warm light on people, one cool color for machines."
