# Sparks (fuel, not outcomes)

> 🎯 SAMPLE TEMPLATE — goals are outcomes; sparks are fuel, the things that refuel you whether or not they produce anything. If a whole month passes with none of them touched, that is drift into all-work mode, and the right nudge is not another work session. Replace the `(sample)` bullets with yours.
>
