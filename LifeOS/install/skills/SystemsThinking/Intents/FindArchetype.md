# FindArchetype

A match of the observed behavior to a systems archetype, verified against the system's own structure, with the canonical intervention adapted to context.

## Ideal State

- The behavior description covers the observed behavior in 2–4 sentences naming the actors, the time period, and prior fixes.
- The match rests on mechanism and delay structure: the system's specific CLD is verified against the archetype's structure.
- The canonical intervention is adapted into a first move, a success measure, and a reevaluation date.
- The intervention addresses the underlying loop and delays.
- Evidence of comparable interventions, where available, is cited as strengthening the match; missing evidence or contradictory timing is stated as weakening it.
- The output checks which other archetype the intervention might activate.
- Composite and novel structures are reported as valid outcomes.

## Anti-criteria

- Similar symptoms alone are never sufficient for a match.
- No forced match: a forced or absent match routes to `CausalLoop.md` instead of being reported as an archetype.

## Output format

```
🧩 ARCHETYPE MATCH: [name]

BEHAVIOR DESCRIPTION:
[What is happening, 2-4 sentences]

WHY THIS ARCHETYPE:
- Signature: [structural signature that matches]
- Evidence: [observable pattern]
- Dynamic: [reinforcing + balancing loops involved]

CANONICAL INTERVENTION:
[From archetype documentation]

ADAPTATION TO CONTEXT:
- First move: [specific action]
- Measure of success: [how you'll know it's working]
- Timeline: [when to re-evaluate]

RISK: [which other archetype could be mimicked — second-order check]
```
