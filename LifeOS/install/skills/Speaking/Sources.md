# Speaking Opportunity Sources

The kinds of source FindOpportunities checks, what each one yields, and how often. The actual speakers, conferences and bureaus to check live in Config.md (SKILL.md § Data & Config); this file is the method.

## Speaker pages

People in your field who publish where they speak (`## Speaker pages to monitor` in Config.md). Where they speak points to conferences that pay speakers in your topic areas: a monitored speaker appearing at an event is evidence that the event pays. Their event pages also surface conferences you did not know about, which become new Config.md entries. The `## Highest-yield source` entry is checked first on every run.

## Conferences

Events grouped by pay tier (`## Conferences` in Config.md):

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tier 1: Premium paid keynotes | Pays at or above the top of your fee range |
| Tier 2: Paid or honorarium | Pays inside your fee range, or a fixed honorarium |
| Tier 3: Prestige or exposure | May or may not pay; tracked only when its prestige justifies an unpaid slot, and flagged unpaid or fee unknown |

Priority targets are events worth checking directly for CFP status even when no aggregator lists them; many invite-led events never post a public CFP and are reached through a nomination form or the booker.

## Speaker bureaus

Bureaus (`## Speaker bureaus` in Config.md) represent paid speakers and show which events book through them. Check them for new representation and for the events their rosters are booked at.

## CFP aggregators

Public boards that list open calls for proposals. These cover most fields:

- **Sessionize**: sessionize.com (tech CFPs)
- **PaperCall**: papercall.io (developer conferences)
- **CFP Land**: cfpland.com (aggregator)

Add the boards for your own niche under `## Extra CFP aggregators` in Config.md.

## Update frequency

- **Speaker pages**: Check monthly
- **Conference CFPs**: Check weekly during CFP season (usually 3-6 months before event)
- **Speaker bureaus**: Check quarterly for new representation
