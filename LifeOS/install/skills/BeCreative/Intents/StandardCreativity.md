# StandardCreativity

One polished answer to a creative request, surfaced from five diverse Verbalized Sampling candidates.

## Ideal State

- The response is one answer, polished and refined: the least-obvious of five Verbalized Sampling candidates sampled from the tails (asked for at p<0.10, though the printed probabilities are never the evidence) that still fully satisfies the request, and the response names the runner-up it beat.
- The five candidates are genuinely distinct from each other, not rephrasings of one idea.
- The surfaced answer is one the principal would not have written on the first pass, still fully answers the brief, and says why it beats the obvious answer.

## Anti-criteria

- The answer never trades away part of the request to be less obvious.
- The response is never the list of five in place of the one chosen, polished answer.
