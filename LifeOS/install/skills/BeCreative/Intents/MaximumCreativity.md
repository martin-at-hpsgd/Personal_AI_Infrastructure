# MaximumCreativity

The most genuinely novel of five radically different Verbalized Sampling candidates, elaborated in full.

## Ideal State

- The response is the most genuinely novel of five radically different Verbalized Sampling candidates sampled from the tails (asked for at p<0.10, though the printed probabilities are never the evidence), and the response names the runner-up it beat.
- The five reach deeper into the low-probability tails than StandardCreativity: each is genuinely non-formulaic, not a variation on a common answer.
- The five span different formats, framings, or genres.
- The chosen direction is elaborated, not just named.
- The surfaced answer is one the principal would not have written on the first pass, still fully answers the brief, and says why it beats the obvious answer.

## Anti-criteria

- The winner is never a stock or cliched answer to the request.
