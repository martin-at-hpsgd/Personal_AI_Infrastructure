# HillClimb preferences

> Optional. Read as prose by the HillClimb skill (`skills/HillClimb/SKILL.md` § Data & Config); an entry here switches on an optional behaviour in `Intents/Ideate.md` and states the user's own words behind it. There is no fixed schema; delete an entry to turn its behaviour off. A `History.md` beside it may record the full stories of past climbs; the skill runs the same without either file.

- **The DA ideates first (Alex Example, 2026-01-15).** Before any proposer roster runs, the DA reads the train misses itself and persists its own generation.
