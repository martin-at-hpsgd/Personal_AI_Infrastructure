# Iceberg

An iceberg analysis that walks from specific events down through pattern and generating structure to the mental models that sustain it.

## Ideal State

- Specific events (date, time, scope) are connected to an evidenced pattern over a stated window, at least three candidate generating structures, and the beliefs sustaining them.
- Frequency, trigger conditions, and adjacent-system recurrence distinguish the pattern as recurring, escalating, shifting, or seasonal.
- Candidate structures cover relevant loops, incentives, information/resource/authority flows, delays, thresholds, ownership gaps, rules, or resource allocation.
- The primary generator passes the counterfactual: remove the symptom while leaving this structure intact, and a similar symptom would appear elsewhere.
- Mental models are inferred from what the system structurally rewards, trusts, treats as scarce, and optimizes over time (in a solo AI harness, what its doctrine, hooks, meters and the principal's corrections reward), naming who or what holds the belief and the evidence that could shift it.
- Each layer has an intervention candidate, and culture or doctrine changes have a concrete first move.
- The recommendation weighs cost and feasibility; an unexecutable mental-model change is not a finished intervention.

## Anti-criteria

- A deploy correlation alone is never presented as a generator; it is a pattern, not a generator.
- Individual opinions alone are never offered as the mental model.
- Recurrence is never silently accepted: an event patch is legitimate only with the deferred structural fix named and on the roadmap.
- An individual is never treated as the structure; the requirements and incentives acting on them are named instead.

## Inputs

- A behavior that recurs, with its events and whatever measurements the request carries.
- No recurring pattern: hand off to **RootCauseAnalysis/Postmortem**.

## Output format

```
🧊 ICEBERG ANALYSIS: [topic]

EVENTS (Layer 1):
- [Specific event 1]
- [Related event 2]
- ...

PATTERN (Layer 2):
- Time window: [e.g., 3 recurrences in 6 weeks]
- Shape: [recurring / escalating / shifting / seasonal]
- Trigger conditions: [what predicts it]

STRUCTURE (Layer 3):
- Primary generator: [feedback loop / incentive / flow / delay / rule]
- Contributing structures: [list]
- Test: if we remove the symptom, would this structure produce another?

MENTAL MODEL (Layer 4):
- Belief that makes the structure feel correct: [...]
- Who holds it: [...]
- What evidence would shift it: [...]

INTERVENTION CANDIDATES:
- Event-layer patch: [quick fix, explicitly deferred]
- Structural fix: [the real lever]
- Mental-model shift: [the durable change]

RECOMMENDED: [which layer to target given cost/benefit]
```
