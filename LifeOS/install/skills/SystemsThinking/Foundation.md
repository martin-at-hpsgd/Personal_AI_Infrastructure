# SystemsThinking — Foundation

Consult from Iceberg or CausalLoop when boundaries or qualitative dynamics are uncertain.

- Contested variable definitions or system boundaries make a precise-looking CLD misleading. Expose the disagreement before treating the diagram as shared.
- Oscillation can come from balancing feedback with delay; it is not evidence of reinforcement by itself.
- Informal communication and actual rewards may generate behavior contrary to org charts, written policy or written doctrine.
- A CLD alone does not determine quantitative behavior. Stocks add inertia: accumulated quantities change through flows, so intervention effects need time.
- Mental-model shifts may require experienced anomalies or demonstrated alternatives, not argument alone.
- When structural action is infeasible, an achievable parameter intervention can be the correct recommendation.
