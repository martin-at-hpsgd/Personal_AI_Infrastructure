# Taste profile

Read by the MediaTaste skill before any recommendation (`skills/MediaTaste/SKILL.md` (not in the public release) § Data & Config). Hand-edited; replace the made-up examples with your own. The database beside it, `media.jsonl`, is created by `Tools/Media.ts` on your first `add`; `skills/MediaTaste/Setup.md` (not in the public release) § Fill in covers both.

## Anchors

- Example Saga (book): a clever magic system and a hero who wins by planning.
- Harbor Nights (show): a slow-burn mystery with a funny ensemble.

## What works

- Progression that is earned, with costs the hero actually pays.
- Humor that does not undercut the stakes.

## Avoid

- Padded middle volumes.
- Plots that depend on characters not talking to each other.
